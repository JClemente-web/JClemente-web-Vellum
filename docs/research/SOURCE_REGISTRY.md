## Regulatory facts frozen for the architecture

From SRC-ANAC-RBAC100-HTML, not from memory:

- Categories in 100.5: Aberta (weight in flight ≤ 25 kg, VLOS or EVLOS, up to 120 m / 400 ft AGL, areas distant from third parties, operational risk assessment with the 150 m or mechanical-barrier exception and a 12-month currency), Específica (when any open requirement fails, plus Subpart B), Certificada (dangerous goods that can create high third-party risk, or when ANAC finds the risk cannot be mitigated without certification).
- Specific operations (100.103): a published standard scenario, or an ANAC-acceptable risk method plus operational authorization.
- Standard scenario (100.3): issued by Instrução Suplementar.
- One remote pilot operates one UA at a time unless ANAC authorizes otherwise (100.23(b)).
